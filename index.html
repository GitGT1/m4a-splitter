1 Recording

Fastest: in your recorder app open the recording and tap Share → Splitter (install this app with Chrome first). Or tap above and pick the file from Drive or Downloads. Recordings in Android/data are hidden by Android; use Share instead.

    2 Split

    One tap: cuts into parts of about 10 min on pauses, then sends them to Groq for the transcript.
